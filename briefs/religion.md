## Proposed scope (300–450 words)
1. **Definition**: no single agreed definition. Give a working definition, then the main ways philosophers define religion: substantive (by what is believed, e.g. supernatural beings) and functional (by what it does for people and communities), each with its difficulty (too narrow / too broad). Source: SEP "The Concept of Religion".
2. **Why the definition matters here**: not every religion centres on a creator god; some traditions are non-theistic (sourced example). The four arguments in this section concern God as conceived in classical theism (link `god`).
3. **Religion, belief and reason**: religious belief vs religious practice and community; the topic's focus is on the *arguments* about a god's existence and nature. Faith and reason in one or two neutral sentences (fideism vs evidentialism named only if sourced).
4. **Australian context** (section Q8): one sentence with ABS 2021 Census figures on religious affiliation, if wanted.
5. **Related**: god, ontological/cosmological/teleological arguments, problem-of-evil, morality (Unit 3), pseudoscience (Section 8: religion and science).
