## Proposed scope (450–600 words)
1. **Senses of "good"**: moral and non-moral (a good knife vs a good person); intrinsic and instrumental good. Good as a normative term (link `descriptive-normative`, `is-ought`; `appeal-to-nature` for "natural" ≠ "good", not repeated).
2. **God and goodness (Unit 2)**: the Euthyphro dilemma: is something good because God commands it, or does God command it because it is good? Plato, *Euthyphro* 10a (Jowett), quoted with a gloss. Each horn's difficulty, and the main reply (goodness grounded in God's nature) with its critics' objection. Divine command theory named.
3. **Good in ethics (Unit 3, short)**: moral theories differ on what makes something good or right; forward links to utilitarianism, Kantian ethics, virtue ethics, value, morality. No comparison of theories (section Q6).
4. **Questions to explore** (section Q7): nature of morality; morality and religion.
5. **Related**: evil, benevolence, god, descriptive-normative, is-ought, appeal-to-nature, value, morality, ethics.
