## Proposed scope (400–550 words)
1. **Definition and usage** (section Q5): "god" as a general term (a being worshipped as divine); "God" as the name of the God of classical theism (Judaism, Christianity, Islam). Polytheism, monotheism.
2. **Classical theism**: God as a perfect being: omnipotent, omniscient, perfectly good (links to each term), creator and sustainer of everything else, eternal, necessary. Anselm's "that than which nothing greater can be conceived" quoted once here or only in the ontological entry (to decide at drafting; not both).
3. **Positions on God's existence**: theism, atheism, agnosticism; deism and pantheism in one line each. Neutral definitions only.
4. **Coherence of the concept**: the attributes can seem to conflict with one another or with the world (omnipotence paradoxes; foreknowledge and free will; problem of evil); links, not discussion.
5. **Questions to explore** (section Q7): 2–3 on the nature of a god or gods.
6. **Related**: religion, omnipotence, omniscience, benevolence, good, the four arguments.
