## Proposed scope (150–350 words)
1. **Definition** (Bland pp. 8, 13): the proposition an argument tries to establish; the premises are offered in its support.
2. **Finding the conclusion in prose**: conclusion indicator words (therefore, so, thus, hence, it follows that, consequently); it is often stated first (Penny's argument, Bland p. 13), and sometimes not stated at all (hidden conclusion, Bland p. 16; link).
3. **Main and intermediate conclusions** (Bland p. 15): an intermediate conclusion (IC) is supported by premises and then supports the main conclusion; it adds no new information.
4. **A conclusion is not the same as a summary or an opinion**: what makes it a conclusion is its role in the argument.
5. **Related**: premise, argument, claim, standard form, hidden premise.

Out of scope for this entry (linked instead): the logical relation (entailment, inductive support) in detail: section 2.
