## Proposed scope (400–550 words)
1. **Two uses**: broad (anything bad: suffering, harm, wrongdoing; the sense in the problem of evil) and narrow (extreme moral wrongdoing, as in "an evil act"; the sense debated in ethics).
2. **Moral and natural evil**: suffering caused by free agents vs by natural events (disease, earthquakes). Examples constructed or historical-neutral (e.g. a named natural disaster, sourced).
3. **Evil as privation**: Augustine: evil is not a thing but the absence of a good that should be there. Short quotation if a PD translation is confirmed; gloss; main objection (pain seems real, not an absence) with the reply.
4. **Evil in ethics (Unit 3, short)**: whether "evil" names something beyond "very wrong"; forward links to suffering, morality, ethics.
5. **Related**: good, problem-of-evil, benevolence, suffering, morality.
