## Proposed scope (300–450 words)
1. **Definition**: goodwill; willing and acting for the good of others. Omnibenevolence: perfect or unlimited goodness. Distinguish God's moral goodness (benevolence towards creatures) from goodness as perfection (God as the standard of good), with a link to `good`.
2. **What a perfectly benevolent being would want**: the premise in the problem of evil that a good being prevents suffering as far as it can; the qualification "unless it has a morally sufficient reason" (link `problem-of-evil`).
3. **Related**: good, evil, god, omnipotence, omniscience, problem-of-evil.
