## Proposed scope (350–500 words)
1. **Definition**: knowing all truths (and believing no falsehoods). Refinements named briefly: whether it includes knowledge of the future.
2. **Foreknowledge and free will**: if God knows today what you will choose tomorrow, can you choose otherwise? Set out in standard form; then the main responses, each paired with its objection: Boethius (God is outside time: knowledge is not *fore*knowledge), the reply that knowing an action does not cause it, and open theism (the future free actions are not yet there to be known). Forward link `free-will`, `determinism` (Section 9).
3. **Why it matters**: omniscience in the problem of evil (God knows of all suffering).
4. **Related**: god, omnipotence, benevolence, problem-of-evil, free-will, determinism.
