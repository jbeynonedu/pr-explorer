## John's unit material (added to the Project 27 Nov 2026: "Religion OneNote")

What bears on the entries (teaching notes, not sources; every claim still sourced at Sourced):
- **Week 1**: Omni-God and perfect being theology (Anselm); omni-attributes plus eternal, immaterial, omnipresent; paradox of the stone with the logically-possible reply; moral vs natural evil. Faith vs reason: Kierkegaard (1813–1855), *Fear and Trembling* (1843), "leap of faith", Abraham, "by virtue of the absurd". **Pascal's Wager** (*Pensées* §233 / Lafuma 418, Trotter trans.; payoff table; pragmatic, not an argument for existence; cultivating belief). **Flew's invisible gardener** ("Theology and Falsification", 1950/1955; "death by a thousand qualifications"; falsification challenge). Toolkit: standard form; valid vs sound; operators (the OneNote uses `>` and `≡`; the Explorer keeps `⊃`); necessary/sufficient examples from the topic; appeal to consequences (Pascal).
- **Week 2 (cosmological)**: contingent vs necessary being; infinite regress; first cause in time vs sustaining cause (Aquinas vs kalam); Aquinas Ways 1–3 (New Advent, Dominican Province trans.); kalam (al-Ghazālī, Craig; Big Bang; quantum events); objections: who made God / special pleading; Hume on causation and on the whole vs its parts (composition); is an actual infinite impossible (Hilbert's hotel, the even-numbers paradox); the gap to God; classification deductive.
- **Week 3**: a priori / a posteriori; Anselm (*Proslogion* 2–4; SJSU text with Gaunilo and reply); chocolate-cake analogy (textbook *On God*); hidden assumption that existence is great-making; Gaunilo's island, Anselm's intrinsic-maximum reply; Kant (100 coins); later defenders (Descartes, Leibniz, Gödel, Plantinga). Paley ch. 1 (heath, stone, watch); fine-tuning (multiverse, anthropic principle; Swinburne); Hume's critique (organism vs machine; designer proportioned to effect, many or imperfect gods; one universe observed; evil); classification table (cosmological a posteriori deductive; ontological a priori deductive; teleological a posteriori inductive).
- **Week 4 (problem of evil)**: Epicurus via Hume X; inconsistent set (four claims, after Job); logical (McCloskey, Mackie) vs evidential; hidden premises (a)–(c); Plantinga's free will defence, morally significant freedom; **defence vs theodicy**; Augustine (free will theodicy), Irenaeus/Hick (soul-making), Swinburne (*Is There a God?* 1996: delegated responsibility, the consent objection, the limit of a finite life); greater-good; Leibniz best possible world; sceptical theism; Rowe's fawn (E1), the P-to-Q inference, the sceptical theist reply and its "proves too much" rejoinder. Stimulus Sources 1–8 (IEP Beebe, Trakakis; Swinburne) referred to but not in the Project.
- **Week 6 (religion and science)**: Darwin vs Paley; Intelligent Design and Kitzmiller v. Dover (2005); laws-of-physics argument; conflict / independence (Gould, NOMA) / dialogue (Lennox); Dawkins–Lennox debate.
