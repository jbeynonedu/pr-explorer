## Proposed scope (350–500 words)
1. **Definition**: being all-powerful. The standard refinement: able to do anything logically possible (Aquinas, *ST* I q. 25 a. 3, quoted if public domain).
2. **The paradox of the stone**: can God make a stone too heavy for God to lift? Set out as a constructive dilemma in an `sf` block (link `constructive-dilemma`; validity checked by code). The main reply (the task is self-contradictory, so failing it is no limit) paired with the objection to that reply.
3. **Other limits proposed**: whether an omnipotent being can do evil (link `benevolence`, `problem-of-evil`), named only.
4. **Why it matters**: omnipotence is one of the three premises of the problem of evil.
5. **Related**: god, omniscience, benevolence, problem-of-evil, constructive-dilemma, necessary-sufficient.
