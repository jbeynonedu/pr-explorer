## "Analytical essays in Philosophy & Reason" (p. 7) EXACT

> When creating responses in Philosophy & Reason, an analytical essay is connected prose that has the purpose of establishing a claim or assertion (central thesis) through careful reasoning. In this respect, essays present an argument. As part of this process, key concepts, arguments and assumptions are clarified, and counterarguments identified and responded to, applying the principle of charity. Language is used to facilitate logical rather than rhetorical persuasion.
